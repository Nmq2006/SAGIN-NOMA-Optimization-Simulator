\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
% =========================================================================

In this monograph, we investigated the joint optimization of flight trajectory and NOMA power allocation in dual-hop SAGIN networks over generalized Rician fading channels. Closed-form analytical expressions for end-to-end outage probability under Partial Relay Selection were rigorously derived using first-order Marcum-Q functions. To solve the non-convex optimization problem under geometric SIC constraints, we developed an iterative SQP Alternating Optimization benchmark and a real-time Actor-Critic DRL policy with Guided Displacement and Logarithmic Reward Shaping. Simulation results verified that the DRL policy achieves near-optimal outage performance while executing within $1.5$ milliseconds, validating its decisive potential for 6G airborne communication platforms.
